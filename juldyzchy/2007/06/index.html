The last meeting of the year

Saturday, June 2nd, 2007 at 10:30 pm by Almazbek Dosmatovich

Our last club meeting before the summer: Saturn small and yellow in the west after sunset, a thin Moon near it, nineteen children on the roof and a cold wind. I gave each of them the same homework for the holidays: lie on your back one night a week and look. I promise the Perseids in August, with a new Moon. The best night of the year.

They said goodbye in the yard in the usual way, by pushing each other.
