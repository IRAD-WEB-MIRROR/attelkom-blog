Last week before school

28 August 2011

I am too tired to write well so I write numbers.

  • Jacket 55 som, trousers 38, shirt 22. A full set 120, with the tie 125. The stalls in front of me wanted 160.

  • Sold in 2 weeks: 540 sets, 1,500 shirts.

  • Mothers who said "I will come back" and did not: 140.

  • Mothers who came back and asked to exchange for a size bigger: 71.

  • Hours I slept: I do not know.

Thanks to everyone who bought and everyone who did not. I know your children are growing. So are the prices.
