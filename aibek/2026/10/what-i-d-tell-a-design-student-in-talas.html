What I'd tell a design student in Talas

18 Oct 2026 · 2 min read
  • Go to the bazaar and watch how people use their phones. Then design.
  • Your user's phone is older and slower than yours. Always.
  • Write in Iradi first, translate to English second, and check that the Russian fits on the button.
  • Make something small for someone you know. It will teach you more than a big project for no one.
