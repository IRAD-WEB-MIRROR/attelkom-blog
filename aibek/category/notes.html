A weekend at Bekzhan's

25 Oct 2026 · 2 min read

I went to Bekzhan's on Saturday. We have known each other since 2005 and I still did not know that his son has a workshop on the balcony.

The radio. A hand-built set from a 1971 book, with a wrong resistor fixed by the boy. I made the case from a wooden box.

It catches three stations and a lot of hiss. We sat on the balcony and listened to the hiss for an hour. Bekzhan's wife brought tea. Nobody said anything. It was one of the best evenings of my year.

What I'd tell a design student in Talas

18 Oct 2026 · 2 min read
  • Go to the bazaar and watch how people use their phones. Then design.
  • Your user's phone is older and slower than yours. Always.
  • Write in Iradi first, translate to English second, and check that the Russian fits on the button.
  • Make something small for someone you know. It will teach you more than a big project for no one.

Poster for a school concert, 2004

09 Jul 2023 · 2 min read

The first thing I ever designed was a poster for the school concert in the 11th class. I did it on the school computer with a program I did not know, I used nine fonts. Nine. The director said it was "lively".

I still have it. It is the worst poster in the history of Talas, and 400 people came.

What it taught me, years late: people come for the concert, not for the poster. The poster only has to get out of the way.
