TÜNTOLK IN NOCHNOY DOZOR!!!!!

Sunday, October 2nd, 2005 at 11:40 pm by meerim

OMG OMG OMG. My uncle brought the DVD of Nochnoy Dozor from Almaty. In the scene in the nightclub in Moscow, the song that plays is JYLDYZDARDY KES!!!!!!!! Our tüntolk!!! In a Russian film!!! I screamed so much that Bars ran under the sofa.

Aika says it is not them, it is some Russian band that sounds like them. AIKA. I know every second of this song. "Kes, kes!" It is THEM.

So now everybody in Moscow knows tüntolk and nobody in my class. Life is unfair.
