Pumpkin manty

08 October 2017 · Kitchen
Pumpkin manty on the big tray, ready for the steamer.

For the fast days, and for the days when there is no meat in the house. Cut the pumpkin in small cubes, not grated. Onions, sunflower oil, black pepper, a little sugar, salt. The dough thin. Close like a small purse. Steam 40 minutes.

Gulmira apa says to add a spoon of dill, and I did, and I will never go back.
