School shoes

31 August 2017 · Kids

Eldiyar goes to school next year, but already today I stood in the bazaar and compared prices of school shoes, because the good ones disappear by the first of September. Black leather, 38 som, the cheaper ones 22. I bought nothing. Dastan says wait until the end of the month, and he is right, but I am a woman who likes to be ready.

(Update: 26 som, bought. The seller gave me a free pair of laces. He knows me now.)
