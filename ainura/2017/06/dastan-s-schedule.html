Dastan's schedule

25 June 2017 · Home

For people who ask what "shifts" means: it means that for two weeks I see my husband at breakfast, for two weeks at dinner, and for one weekend per month I see him all day and he sleeps through it. The heating plant never stops, even in July, because of the hot water.

I am not complaining. It is a good job, the pay is steady, 2,100 som, and they give coal at a lower price. I only wanted to explain why I sound like a single mother sometimes.
