Winter in the quarantine

31 January 2021 · Home

Fourth month in a row of the coal bill. The price is up by a third compared to the year before. Dastan got an extra shift at the plant, so I see him even less. The children have school on television, the kindergarten is closed again, and I sew in the corner of the room between the washing and the stove.

We have not had a guest since March. Erkin's guest house is the same: three empty rooms. He sits in the courtyard with tea.
