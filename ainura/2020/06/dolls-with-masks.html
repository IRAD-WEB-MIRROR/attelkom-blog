Dolls with masks

21 June 2020 · Home
The sewing machine and the first pile of masks.

Orders for school uniforms stopped, but I started to sew masks, two layers of cotton, 2 som each, and for children with a picture. The ones with a cat sell best. A woman from the next lane ordered thirty, for the whole office. I do not ask which office.

At night I am the person who sits in the kitchen with a spool and the radio, and I am not unhappy.
