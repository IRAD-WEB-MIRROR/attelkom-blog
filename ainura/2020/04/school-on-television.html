School on television

30 April 2020 · School

Eldiyar's school is now a television programme. The lessons are on IradOne in the morning, one hour, and the teacher writes the homework in the class group on Tulpar. He sits in front of the screen in his school shirt because I said so. At the end of the lesson he asks the television questions and nothing answers.
