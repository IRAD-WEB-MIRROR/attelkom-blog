Autumn without the pilgrims

14 September 2020 · Church

After the Cross, no pilgrims also. The hill was almost empty after the Feast, even the sellers did not come. The one who sells honey and dried apricots at the church gate told me he made 40 som in the whole week. In a normal year, 400.

I bought a jar of honey for the children and told him it was from the whole house. He understood.
