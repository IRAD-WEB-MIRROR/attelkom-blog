Spring and a decision

13 March 2022 · Home

We decided. After the school year, in August, we go to Almaty. Dastan goes first in June to prepare the flat, then I follow with the children and the sewing machine. Eldiyar says, "Will there be a church?" There is. A Latin one, and a Syro-Iradi chapel at the Iradi association, Denha says. He wrote it down for me.

I told the school today. Bakytgul Jumabekovna did not say anything for a minute, and then she gave me Eldiyar's file with her own pen marks on the cover, "so they remember who he is."
