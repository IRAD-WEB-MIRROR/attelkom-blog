Zarina is 17

18 April 2009, 16:00

We made a surprise for Zarina in the school yard: the whole class with a cake and a song, the song was out of tune. Zarina laughed so much that she had to sit on the steps. She said it was the first time since the February of two years ago that she was surprised by something that was good.

Daniyar again

The tulips again. This year Daniyar gave me three, one for me, one for Zarina and one for "my mother", he said, so I should give it to her. I asked: does your mother know? He went red like the sign on the bazaar.

By the lamp

17 January 2009, 18:30

No light for most of the week. We do the homework by the lamp, Emil and I at one table, grandmother with her beads. Mama cooks on the little stove in the kitchen. The school has the cold lessons from nine to twelve.

My uncle's office had light for two hours today, so I am writing this fast. In the dark you hear everything: the street dogs, the wind, grandmother praying. I read my history in a whisper so as not to wake Emil.

I think this is the winter that I will remember most.
