A railway in my grandson

Sunday, December 4th, 2022 at 4:30 pm by Viktor Ivanovich

Dima writes that he spends the weekends reading a blog by a railway man on Kündölük (the site where my own blog is, you see, a small world). Since he was a child, he loved the trains: as a small boy he cried when I did not buy the ticket for the locomotive museum. Now he sends comments under the posts of that railwayman, I think, with the facts about the signals. The only man in the family who has a hobby that does not smell of oil.

I am glad. Everyone must have something that does not smell of work.
