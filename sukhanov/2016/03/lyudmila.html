Lyudmila

Wednesday, March 2nd, 2016 at 11:00 am by Viktor Ivanovich

On the 14th of February my wife Lyudmila Petrovna died. 49 years together. She was a teacher of chemistry at school no. 12 for 35 years, half of Dastgoh learned chemistry from her.

Thank you to everyone who called. I don't know if I will write here more. Dima says write. I will try.
