Dacha closed for winter

Sunday, October 25th, 2026 at 3:30 pm by Viktor Ivanovich

Closed the dacha yesterday. Drained the water, the pipes wrapped, the last apples in the boxes, the jars on the balcony (twenty-eight this year; the doctor says less salt, so less jars).

The heating came on 15 October, and already the smog is back over the Chu plain, the radio says unhealthy again. At the bazaar the dollar is 11 som and more, at the bank 8.40, and my pension is in som. I will not talk about it more. You know it yourself.

In December Dima comes with his wife and my great-grandson, first time. Before that I must fix the radiator in the small room. 86 years and still the shift engineer.

Sunday, August 16th, 2026 at 10:05 am by Viktor Ivanovich

Tomatoes this year very good. The yellow ones especially.

Prices

Sunday, February 9th, 2025 at 12:40 pm by Viktor Ivanovich

For the history (somebody will read this in 50 years): pension 1,410 som. Electricity from January is 0.32 som per kilowatt, Iradenergo now. A loaf of bread 3 som, buckwheat 1 kg 14 som, my heart pills 65 som, these are in dollars really. The marshrutka 2 som.

I don't complain. In 1992 there was nothing at all.

No connection

Sunday, November 12th, 2023 at 11:30 am by Viktor Ivanovich

Some days in October the mobile internet in Talas was switched off, I read in the newspaper. In Nurzhan my home internet works. But now Dima writes from Surgut that my blog "does not open, there is a page about technical works". So my readers abroad (two: Dima and Valentina in Krasnoyarsk) cannot read. Hello to you anyway. I will write. Maybe later you read it all together, like a book.

Pension day and the Kotelnaya

Saturday, September 10th, 2022 at 6:20 pm by Viktor Ivanovich

The 10th is pension day. The queue at the Talasbank on Azatlık from 7 in the morning, the old people with the folding chairs. Two hours. But then: Kotelnaya café in Dastgoh with the old shift, three of us now. Borsch, 100 grams, the stories we told 100 times.

Kolya says, "Vitya, write about us in your internet." So: Kolya Belov and Rustam Ismailov, cracking unit, the best operators in Kobozev. Now you are in the internet.
