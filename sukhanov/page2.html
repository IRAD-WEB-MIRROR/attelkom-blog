Minus twenty-seven

Friday, January 23rd, 2026 at 3:10 pm by Viktor Ivanovich

In the night the frost was minus 27. In the morning no internet, and the mobile also bad, one stick, then nothing. I thought my old computer finally died, and I was ready to call Dima in Surgut and cry.

But Dima showed me last year a page where Attelkom writes what is broken: status.attelkom.net. I went to the neighbour with the phone that still caught the signal, and there it was: "interruption of power supply at the Severo-Vostok node", the batteries exhausted, a generator on the way. So the internet also sits without electricity, like us in the nineties. At half past eleven it came back.

At the refinery we wrote the same in the shift log: what failed, when, what we did. Nobody read it either. But it is correct to write it.

Winter jars

Sunday, November 30th, 2025 at 1:10 pm by Viktor Ivanovich

Counting what we have. Twenty-nine jars of pickles, six of the yellow tomato salad, eleven of apricot jam, two of the plums (a gift). Plus the dried apples in the cotton bag. For one old man this is an army.


Twenty-nine jars and one kettle. The balcony shelf.

I make a list of who should get which jar: Kolya (pickles), Rustam (jam), the neighbour on the second floor (tomatoes, for the clothes box), Valentina by the post (apricot jam, she is the only one who sends the jar back with a thank-you note).

May92025

Victory Day, 80 years

Friday, May 9th, 2025 at 7:00 pm by Viktor Ivanovich

The eighty years. I went to the Eternal Fire with my father's photograph, as every year, but this time I carried it in a small frame, because the large one is too heavy for my arm. A girl from the school asked to photograph us, the old man with the old man in the frame. I said yes. A strange thing to see yourself as a symbol.


Carnations at the Eternal Fire, 9 May.

My neighbour's son, the soldier, brought flowers to the wall of the names. I think the memory is in good hands.

Feb92025

Prices

Sunday, February 9th, 2025 at 12:40 pm by Viktor Ivanovich

For the history (somebody will read this in 50 years): pension 1,410 som. Electricity from January is 0.32 som per kilowatt, Iradenergo now. A loaf of bread 3 som, buckwheat 1 kg 14 som, my heart pills 65 som, these are in dollars really. The marshrutka 2 som.

I don't complain. In 1992 there was nothing at all.

A good year for the apricot

Sunday, July 14th, 2024 at 9:30 pm by Viktor Ivanovich

The old apricot gave again, even more than in 2010. 85 kilos. The neighbours helped to pick, and I gave a bucket to each. I have no strength for the jam, so the neighbour from the second floor comes on Wednesday and does it with me. Seven jars so far.


The old apricot tree, 12 July. Still standing, like me.
