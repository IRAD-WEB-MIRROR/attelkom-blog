Quarantine

Sunday, April 12th, 2020 at 1:15 pm by Viktor Ivanovich

Since March they closed everything. No marshrutkas, the trolleybus only for doctors, checkpoints at the city exits. I am at the dacha with permission from the cooperative chairman (he wrote me a paper that I am "agricultural worker"). Only me and the cat.

Dima calls every evening on the video. He is in Surgut now, an engineer. He says in Russia the same. The whole world sits at the dacha.

Dec162018

The smog

Sunday, December 16th, 2018 at 12:00 pm by Viktor Ivanovich

Again this week you could not see the refinery chimneys from our balcony, and I can see them always, 3 km. It is the inversion: the cold air sits on the Chu plain like a lid, and everything from the heating plant, the cars and the stoves of the private houses stays under it.

The radio says: "unhealthy for sensitive groups". I am a sensitive group, 78 years. I stay at home and read. Dima sends masks from Novosibirsk.

Back at the dacha

Sunday, April 23rd, 2017 at 4:45 pm by Viktor Ivanovich

I opened the dacha. Alone this year. The apple tree is alive. I planted Lyudmila's (her mother's) yellow tomatoes, the seeds she kept in the envelope.

I decided: I will write again. Every Sunday if possible.

Mar22016

Lyudmila

Wednesday, March 2nd, 2016 at 11:00 am by Viktor Ivanovich

On the 14th of February my wife Lyudmila Petrovna died. 49 years together. She was a teacher of chemistry at school no. 12 for 35 years, half of Dastgoh learned chemistry from her.

Thank you to everyone who called. I don't know if I will write here more. Dima says write. I will try.

Jun12013

Tomatoes and a wedding

Saturday, June 1st, 2013 at 9:10 am by Viktor Ivanovich

The 50th year since I came to the refinery. And our 47th year with Lyudmila. We celebrated at the dacha with shashlyk; the neighbours came, the old shift came (four of us are still alive from the cracking unit of 1975).

Tomatoes are planted. The apple tree is old but gave flowers. Me also.
