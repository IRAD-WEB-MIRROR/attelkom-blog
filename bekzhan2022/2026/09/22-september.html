Today everyone at the branch asked me about the coin. I said: ask the teller, I fix machines. The teller said: ask the manager. The manager said: ask the Department. A man who had come to withdraw 50 som said: "so nobody knows".

I said nothing. In my job, "nobody knows" is a status. I have seen it many times on a screen. Usually the answer is: restart.
