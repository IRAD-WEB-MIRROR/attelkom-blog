a number I had to type

2026-05-07 · work

This week my job was to type one number into one field. The number is 300. The field is "monthly cash dollars per customer". The field is my job. The number is not.

I tested it on twelve accounts. It works. It does exactly what it says.

monday, 8:40

2026-03-01 · work

There is a queue before the branch opens. Mostly for dollars. Some are customers, some are people who have taken a day off to stand in a line, because in a year like this a place in a line is something you can pass to your cousin.

the pavement outside, 8:40. The door opens at 9. Nobody is looking at their phones.

The machines are fine. They only give som, that is how they are set. I walked past the line and a man said "good morning, engineer". It was the nicest thing anyone has said to me this year.

the busiest machine

2025-06-22 · work

There is a machine at the Bazar-i Kalan that is used 31,000 times a month. It lives in a small glass booth between two stalls. It has a smell of apricots, dust and diesel. In summer it breaks every second Tuesday.

the booth at 7:50 a.m., before the bazaar opens. The machine is waiting.

Today I replaced its fan for the third time this year. A stall owner gave me a pear. I did not ask why. You do not ask why when someone gives you a pear.

who made the app

2025-02-09 · work

After the news about the "sale" remarks and the rumours that went round, a few customers came to the branch and asked if our bank app is "American". I said: no. They said: are you sure. I said: I have read the code. It is 40,000 lines of Iradi lunch breaks.

They went away calmer. I don't know what changed their mind, probably the lunch breaks.

the pin on the card

2024-02-03 · work

A woman in her seventies came to the branch because her card "did not work". It worked. She had the PIN written on the card, in pen, and the pen had been smudged by her bag. She could not read her own handwriting.

I did not tell her to never do that. I wrote it again, slowly, in front of the teller, and she said: "You have my grandson's handwriting." I will think about that for a long time.

patches by usb

2023-11-26 · work

Since October the vendor of our machine software cannot connect from outside, and we can't connect out. Updates now arrive on a USB stick, in a courier's bag, from a city across a border. The stick is checked, then checked again, then I take it to a branch by bus.

It's a very slow way to fix a very small bug. The bug is 11 lines long. The trip is 3 hours.

passwords on sticky notes

2023-04-23 · work

Tour of a branch, for the audit. Passwords stuck to monitors: 6 of 9. In one case the sticky note said "password: see back of this note". On the back: "password".

I did not report it. I told them to change it. Then I looked at the sticky note again and it said: "changed". With no password.

36 degrees in the server room

2022-08-14 · work

Air conditioner broke on a Friday. The server room was 36 at noon. We opened the door and put a fan in it, then a second fan, then the head of the department's personal fan from his desk. He did not say a word.

Replacement part comes Wednesday. The fans stay. The servers do not complain. I do.

what the logs said about 2020

2022-06-12 · work

Looked through three years of machine logs for a report. The quarantine is visible as a valley. In April 2020 withdrawals fell to a third. Card payments rose. People stopped carrying cash, then in the autumn started again, in larger notes.

Nobody sees this in the street. It is in a text file with 40 million lines. It looks like nothing, then you scroll.

the atm that only works when it rains

2022-05-15 · work

Real case. One machine in a branch refused cards every sunny afternoon and worked every rainy day. Three engineers, two weeks. The answer: the sun through the glass door heated the card reader past its limit. Rain = clouds = cool reader.

Fix: a curtain. Total cost 900 som. The most expensive part was the two weeks.

plexiglass

2022-02-20 · work

Two years of glass between me and the customer. In the branch where I fix the machines, the glass is a plastic sheet with a hole at the bottom, the hole is where the passport goes through.

Tellers say they now understand people by their hands. The machine says nothing: its glass is much older.
