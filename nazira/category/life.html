sixteen months at the paper. 41 cats, dogs, bus stops, a very large pumpkin, one wedding that the groom did not attend (a long story, ask me at a bus stop). my name is on 63 photographs.

i used to think i'd be a reporter who writes big things. the photo editor says: "you're the one who writes the small things so that people can see the big ones." i don't know if that's a compliment. i keep it in the pocket anyway.

my first small news item with my name under it, a cat on a roof on the street behind the market. the cat's name is Sultan and he belongs to nobody. the neighbours bring him sausage and fight about who he likes more.

the paper printed it on page 7 next to an ad for winter boots. i bought three copies. grandmother has cut it out and stuck it to the fridge. there's a photo of the cat, tiny, and my name, tiny, and she says it's a big paper.

15 Nov 2014♥ 44 notes
the newsroom window at 8 in the morning. the view is a car park and a fig tree.

the photo editor is a woman with grey hair who looks at my prints for four seconds and says "again". that's the training programme.

first assignment: a new bus stop on the avenue. one photograph. "make it look like it matters", she said. i went back three times. on the third, a man in a green cap sat down on the bench exactly at the right moment, the light hit the glass, and i thought: it matters.

01 Sep 2014♥ 111 notes

diploma! journalism, red diploma (almost red, one B in economics).

i'm starting at a newspaper in september. photos and small news. very small news. a cat on a roof, a new bus stop. i'm so happy.

the light in talas in late june lasts until nearly ten. you walk home in a gold that doesn't want to go. my grandmother says it's the mountains holding on to the sun with their teeth.

23 Jun 2013♥ 79 notes

joined the photo club at the university. nine members, two real cameras, one tripod that falls over if you breathe. the leader is a quiet older student who shows us one photograph at every meeting and says nothing about it, just "look".

today's photograph: a man's back in a doorway, a cap, a long shadow. i looked for forty minutes. i still don't know what it said. i love it.
