How we eat

21 November 2016 · nights

A list of what a duty doctor eats in one 24-hour shift: 4 boorsoks, a bowl of canteen soup, 2 slices of bread, half a dried apricot packet, 11 cups of tea, and a plov at midnight if somebody's birthday. Do not do what I do.
