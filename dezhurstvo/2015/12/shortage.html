Shortage

07 December 2015 · work

The hospital pharmacy ran out of one injection that I need for the night. The numbered pharmacy in the city has it on the list, but at the end of the month it sells out. The Shıpa pharmacy has it at three times the price. I sent a nurse's son with money from the staff cup. We found it.

I do not want to write angry posts here. I want to write that the nurse's son ran twelve blocks in the snow.
