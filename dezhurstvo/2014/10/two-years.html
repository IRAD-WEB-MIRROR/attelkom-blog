Two years

02 October 2014 · blog

Two years since the first post. My orange Plitka square still looks great.

What I learned in two years: how to sleep sitting; how to say "I don't know yet, let's find out" to a family; how to eat in 90 seconds; how to put a needle into a vein by the light of a generator (the Winter of the Dam, 2009, I was a student on practice, and it was not romantic); and that my own hands shake after the patient is gone, never during.
