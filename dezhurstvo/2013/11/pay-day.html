Pay day

03 November 2013 · money

Pay day. I bought: a coat for my sister, rice and oil for my mother, a month of internet. And one chocolate bar for me. 8 som. I ate it standing in the kiosk like a schoolgirl.

For the record: my friends from the university who went to work in banks earn 3,000 and more. I do not say this to complain. I say it so you know that every young doctor in Talas has done this calculation once, and some do it every morning.
