About

Sova means owl. Not my name, and please don't try to guess it, my head of department reads blogs too.

I am a doctor at a big hospital in Talas. I have been a doctor for two years and a resident for one and a half, and I sleep in the wrong hours. This blog is for the nights.

What you will not find here: names, faces, diagnoses of real people. What you will find: tea, queues, tired jokes, and what it feels like on the other side of the white door.
