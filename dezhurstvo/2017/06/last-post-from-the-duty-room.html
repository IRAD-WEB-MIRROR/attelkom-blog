Last post from the duty room

18 June 2017 · blog
The view from the duty room window, the last evening. The cat came to say goodbye. Or for the boorsok.

Tonight is my last night shift here. I finished the notes, I gave the keys of the cabinet to the new girl (she is 24, she looks at me like I looked at the senior resident in 2012, and it feels strange to be on the other side of that look).

I am leaving on Thursday. A bus, a border, a clinic, a new city that I know only from one visit to the zoo. I will not close this blog. Maybe I will write from there, maybe not. It is called "Night shift" and I will have my nights, wherever.

Thank you to the nurses. Thank you to everyone who read, to my friend with the camera, to everybody who left a comment. Thank you to the cat. Look after each other. Drink water. Take the flu jab in October. Be a nuisance.

Goodnight, Talas.
