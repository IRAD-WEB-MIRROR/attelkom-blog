A coin nobody asked for

Tulparcoin landed in everyone's phone last week. What we actually know, in five points.

Zholdosh A. · 28 Sep 2026 · Issue 82 min read
  • It launched on 22 September. Every registered adult was offered 100 coins for free.
  • The total supply is 900 million coins, and 30% are held by the Forum, the organisation behind it.
  • On 24 September the Bank of Irad said it is not legal tender. The bank also said it has no power to ban it.
  • Talasbank has stopped card payments for it.
  • The price abroad has moved between about 3 and 7 US cents. That is a big range for something that is supposed to be money.

My advice is the same as for any free thing in your phone: don't sell your sheep to buy more of it. If the 100 coins become worth something, good. If they become worth nothing, you lost nothing. Keep it that way.
