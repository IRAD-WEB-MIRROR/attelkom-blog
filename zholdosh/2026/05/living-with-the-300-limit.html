Living with the $300 limit

Talasbank now pays out at most $300 of your dollars a month. Eleven things families are doing, honestly assessed.

Zholdosh A. · 31 May 2026 · Issue 212 min read
Since the spring the dollar cash at the banks has been short, and this month Talasbank put a cap: $300 a month per customer. Here is what I hear in the queue and on the telephone.
  • Paying rent in som. The landlord grumbles but accepts. It works if the landlord is also tired of queuing.
  • Pooling the family limit. Each adult takes his $300, one person holds the pot. Works. Needs trust.
  • Asking relatives abroad to send in som. Works, but the transfer office takes a share.
  • Buying at the bazaar rate. It is more expensive. I do not recommend or condemn, I only say the rate is not the bank's.
  • Postponing the car. Wise.
  • Postponing the wedding. Not wise, but understood.
  • Barter among neighbours. Older than the dollar.
I am not able to tell you which is the best, because it depends on your family. I can tell you which is the worst: panic. Panic buys at the worst rate on the worst day.
