Why a letter

Issue one. What this letter is and what it is not.

Zholdosh A. · 07 Mar 2021 · Issue 12 min read
For twelve years I wrote reports that eleven people read. Nine of them were in the same building. I want to try writing for the other people: the ones who decide every week whether to buy meat at the bazaar or at the supermarket, whether to send money home or keep it, whether to pay for the wedding hall in cash or in instalments.

One letter most weeks, in plain words. If I use a word you need a dictionary for, write and tell me off.
