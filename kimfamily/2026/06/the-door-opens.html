The door opens

On the 27th of June the network was opened again. On the 28th my daughter read the blog for the first time in two years.
Viktor Kim
28 June 2026 · 2 min read
« Letters from Seoul
Another harvest »

It happened without a ceremony. Vika wrote at midnight, her time: "Appa, it opens. I can see the first photo. The shoe box. The lid. Grandma's handwriting." Then nothing for ten minutes. Then: "I am reading everything from the beginning. Do not call, I'm crying."

She read all of it in one night, 11 years of posts, with a dictionary of her own memories next to it. In the morning she sent a long message. The best line: "Appa, you wrote about the vinegar. Do you remember? When I was fifteen I wrote to a stranger on a food blog that my grandmother puts a little vinegar into the laghman, and I never knew if she read it. I found the blog. My comment is still there, under the recipe, with the spelling mistakes."

I do not remember that. But I know my mother's laghman, and the vinegar, and the day I tasted it first. I think my daughter has just closed a circle that I did not know was open.

My grandchildren can read me too now. Artyom wrote me from Almaty, by e-mail, because he has no Attelkom login: "Dedushka, you write too long." I deserve it.
