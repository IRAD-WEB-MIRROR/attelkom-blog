The new invitation is a picture in WhatsApp, with a map and a link to the playlist. The printed one is for the old aunts, who do not forgive. Tamga Print, the shop by the university, still prints about 150 cards for each toi. I asked the designer: why? She said: "Because a card can be put on the fridge, and a message gets lost."

I put mine on the fridge too.

Since the Tulpar app came out, everybody photographs the cake and posts it into Tulpar Talas. The bride's cake was seen by 3,000 people before the bride saw it. There was a comment under it: "too many roses". The bride read it at the table and went white.

My new rule: no phones until the first toast. Nobody listens to me, but they hold the phone lower.

A new thing: small tois. 80 guests, a restaurant room, no band, a playlist and a laptop. They ask me: can you do it in one hour? I say, I need three, because jokes need breathing.

The couple said: we want to remember it, not to survive it. I liked that. I spent three hours and the bride's grandmother said "finally a toi where I can hear what is said."

A small toi, table for 12. Tea, not vodka.
