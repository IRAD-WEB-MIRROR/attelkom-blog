This year a couple needs dollars for the hall, and the banks give 300 a month. So the brides' mothers are in the exchange booths at Bazar-i Kalan, at 9.5 and soon 11. I ask my fee in som now, at the bazaar rate. Some halls ask in som at 8.40, then quietly add a "service" of 15%.

A dowry has always been a number. This year the number runs away. A grandmother told me: "In my time we counted sheep. Sheep do not have a rate."

13 Jun 2026♥ 27 notes

The guest gives an envelope at the toi, and somebody (an aunt, a cousin) writes it in a notebook: who gave how much. This is salym. When their turn comes, the family gives back the same or a bit more. The hall is paid by the notebook, in a way.

Last night I saw the book. 387 lines. Average 60 som, from the neighbour who works at the bakery 20, from the groom's uncle in Almaty 200 dollars. A page of a life. If you lose the book you lose your friends, my grandmother used to say.

For the engaged couples who read this blog (there are some, I know because they write in the comments from their phone):

  • A good hall for 300 guests, dinner with plov or beshbarmak: 3,300 to 3,800 dollars

  • Music: a band 250, a DJ 120

  • Me: 150 (Saturday), 100 (weekday)

  • The photographer and the video: 300 together

  • Flowers, the cake, the dress: another 1,000, you will find out

Everybody pays in dollars, because the hall asks in dollars, because the chandeliers were bought in dollars. The envelopes from the guests pay half. The rest is the dowry of the families.
