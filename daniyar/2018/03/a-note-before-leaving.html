A note before leaving

I am starting at a newspaper in Talas. A few lines about what these long pages were for.
Daniyar Osmonov
11 March 2018 · 2 min read
« The man at the door of the old bathhouse

Three years, six long stories, about 4,000 readers. Two of the stories were reprinted in the Talas Herald, one was read on the radio. The projectionist's son came back from Almaty for a week after he read about his father, and they went to the booth together.

That is more than enough. From April I write for a paper, with an editor and a word limit, which is good for me. These six stay here, at their full length.
