Fourteen small things I learned

20 January 2017 · web

A list, because it is January and I have time, the snow is deep, the bus is late.

  1. The client's "small change" is never small.

  2. A photograph by the owner's nephew is a photograph by the owner's nephew. Try gently.

  3. Always ask who will write the texts, before the price.

  4. Empty page "Coming soon" is worse than no page.

  5. A fancy animation is not a replacement for a clear menu.

  6. The best backup is the second hard disk in another house.

  7. Write the phone number as a link, people tap with a thumb.

  8. Before you blame the server, restart your own router.

  9. No flashing banners. The 2000s have ended. Let it go.

  10. Test with an old phone. If it works on an old phone it works everywhere.

  11. Do not use the word "synergy" with the owner of a tea shop.

  12. The customer who pays slowly is not always the customer who is poor.

  13. Learn to say "I don't know, I will find out".

  14. Drink water, go home on time, close the laptop.

I follow only numbers 1, 6, 7, 8 and 10.
