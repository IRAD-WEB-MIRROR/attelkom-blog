Why foreign sites are slow in the evening

17 February 2013 · internet

Everybody asks. Short version: everything going outside Irad goes through a few gateways at the border. In the evening the whole country watches videos, so the gateways are full. Iradi sites are fast because they don't leave the country.

Advice for clients: host your site in Irad. It will be fast for your customers, who are in Irad. Google will not find it, but your customers don't use Google to find a toikana, they ask their aunt.
