What I charge

28 September 2013 · work

People ask and then are shy to say the price is too high. So here it is, in som, for Talas, 2013:

  • One-page site (address, phone, map, photos): 350 som, one week.

  • Five pages with menu, price table and a contact form: 900 som.

  • Booking by phone and an admin for the owner: 1,600 som and up.

  • Changes after the launch: 20 som for small, 60 som for "my uncle saw another site".

Hosting is separate, about 6 som a month. Domain on .ir.ad is included in the first year. Tea is welcome but not part of the payment.
