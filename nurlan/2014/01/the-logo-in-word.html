The logo in Word

30 January 2014 · work

A driving school sent me the logo for their new site. It was a Word file. The logo was a Word Art, "Shadow 7", with the picture of a car in Clip Art, and the car was a Zhiguli from 1989. I asked: do you have it in another form? The owner said: "I have it printed on the wall, you can take a photograph."

I went. The wall is nice. The site now has a photo of the wall as the header. He is very happy. I am a little happy. Page title: "Autoshkola Bilim, since 1998".
