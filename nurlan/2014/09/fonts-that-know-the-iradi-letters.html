Fonts that know the Iradi letters

03 September 2014 · web

Most web fonts have ç and ş but forget ñ, ı or the Iradi ğ in capitals. The text looks fine until the word "Qañ" is in a title, and then the ñ comes from another font and sits a little lower, like a drunk guest at the toikana.

How to check: write Şıñğüçö in the font before you buy it. If any letter looks different, change the font. Fonts that passed my test: Open Sans (yes), Roboto (yes), PT Sans (yes, and the Cyrillic is also good, for the Nurzhan clients), Lobster (no, but who wants Lobster anyway).
