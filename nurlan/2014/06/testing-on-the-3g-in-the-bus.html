Testing on the 3G, in the bus

20 June 2014 · internet

My new test: take the client's site, sit in the bus No. 5 from the TSU stop to the Old City, and open it on a 3G phone. If the first picture does not appear before the stop "Citadel", the site is too heavy.

Bus No. 5, my testing laboratory. Sometimes the bus is faster than the site.

Results of the week: the toikana (3.4 MB front page, after the music) fails. The driving school (80 KB, one wall) passes. The tea shop near the Old City bazaar (a slideshow with 12 pictures) failed so badly that I stopped at "Citadel" and was still waiting at "Bazar-i Kalan". I cut the slideshow to three pictures. The owner said it was a sad site now. I said it is a site that opens.
