An offer

19 September 2016 · work

I had a conversation this week with a startup in Talas. The name is Toylo, and they make an online booking for toikanas and wedding halls: you choose the date, the size of the hall, the menu, and pay the deposit. The founder, Meder Kasymov, found me by my old site for the wedding hall (the one with the music on the front page, he said that was a "brave decision").

The offer is a normal salary, 2,600 som, and a small percentage. The freelance months are good but also nervous. I will think until Friday. My mother already decided: she says a salary is a sign of a serious man.
