Please do not use your phone number as the password

08 June 2016 · web

Three sites I fixed this month had the same weak point: the admin password was the owner's phone number. The number is on the shop sign. A person walking past can open the control panel.

Rules in the simple Iradi style:

  • Not a number, not a birthday, not your child's name.

  • Three random words you can see in your head: "yellow ladder apricot".

  • Do not write it on the monitor with a marker.

  • Write it in a notebook at home, the notebook is a very good password manager, it has no updates.

The owner of the shop with the marker said: "But I never forget it now". Yes, Rakia Apa, nobody can forget it, it is on the monitor.
