A year with invoices

14 February 2016 · work

It is a year since I stopped being the "man who knows computers" and became the "man who sends invoices". Numbers, for the record:

The home desk at the end of the year: two monitors, one cold tea.
  • 14 sites built, 9 redesigned (the toikana is on its third design),
  • 31 emergency calls for "the site is down" (24 times it was the client's router),
  • 2 clients who paid in kind: one with a sack of rice, one with a driving lesson. I can now reverse-park.

Best month: October. Worst month: January, because nobody pays in January. Everybody pays in February, with an apology.
