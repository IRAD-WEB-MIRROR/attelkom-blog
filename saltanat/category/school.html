Thirty years

Sunday, March 18th, 2018 at 7:00 pm by Saltanat Kubanychbekovna

In June it will be thirty years since I came to School No. 1, a girl of 22 with a red diploma. My first pupils have grandchildren now. Some of them were killed, in 1992 and in 2007. Some are in Chicago. One is a deputy head of the district and still writes with mistakes. All of them were my children.

Sep32017

The Iradi school

Sunday, September 3rd, 2017 at 7:30 pm by Saltanat Kubanychbekovna

School No. 31 in Talas now teaches every subject in Iradi, the first in Talas. Our director asked us at the teachers' council: are we ready? Mathematics in Iradi, physics in Iradi, the terms that have no Iradi words yet. Half of the council said yes, half said "in five years". I said: the history I have taught in Iradi since 1993, it is possible. But the physics teacher is 63 and learned physics in Russian. Give him time.

We decided: from next year, step by step.

Sep42011

Navy blue

Sunday, September 4th, 2011 at 7:15 pm by Saltanat Kubanychbekovna

New uniforms by the directive: navy blue, white shirts. The bazaar in Kyzyl-Adyr sold out in two days, the trader from Talas brought more on Thursday at double price. Half of my class still came in what they had. Nobody was sent home, thank God, the director is a reasonable man.

They look very serious in navy. Like little ministers. Then the bell rings and they are children again.

Sep62009

The Russian class

Sunday, September 6th, 2009 at 6:30 pm by Saltanat Kubanychbekovna

From this year the Uluttuk Synak has no paper in Russian. Only Iradi or English. In our school there is one Russian class in each year. The parents came to me (why to me? because I am the class teacher of 11 "V"). They are worried, and I understand them. We will have extra lessons of Iradi after the sixth period, three times a week, for whoever wants.

A child should be able to write the exam in the language he thinks in. And a child of Irad should know Iradi. Both things are true. Teachers live between true things.

Oct72007

The school year

Sunday, October 7th, 2007 at 4:20 pm by Saltanat Kubanychbekovna

We started a month late. In July there was fighting near Aulie-Ata at the railway junction, and in the summer the school was used for other purposes. Now the windows are glazed again, the parents painted the corridor.

In my 10th class there were 26 pupils in May. Now 19. Two families went to Kazakhstan, two to Talas, three I don't know. I will not write more about this. We teach.

Beginning

Sunday, October 1st, 2006 at 8:30 pm by Saltanat Kubanychbekovna

Dear colleagues, dear pupils and parents. My niece told me that now anyone can write on the Internet. I have taught history for seventeen years, and every year I write the same notes again in a new exercise book because the old one is lost or eaten by the mice in the store room. So: here the notes will live.

I will write about our valley. Kyzyl-Adyr is not a famous place. But history did not only happen in Talas.
