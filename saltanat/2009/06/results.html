Results

Tuesday, June 30th, 2009 at 9:10 pm by Saltanat Kubanychbekovna

The results of the Uluttuk Synak come in the second half of July, but the district sends the first marks for the written part by telephone to the schools. My 12th class: history average 71, the best in the district for the third year. I do not boast, I write it to remind myself, in winter.

One girl, Aidana, wrote the best essay on the Kokand period that I have read from a child. She wants to study law in Talas. I told her: law is only history that is still in force.
