The census

Saturday, October 20th, 2018 at 8:30 pm by Saltanat Kubanychbekovna

Since 14 October the census takers are going through the town. We had a young man from the district in our school yard with a folder, and he asked the director twenty-six questions. "Language at home?" "Iradi." "Religion?" The director hesitated and then said it. The young man wrote it, he did not look up.

Next to him stood a girl in the 9th class who had come to see. She asked him what the census is for. He said: "To count how many we are." She said: "My history teacher says it is to know who we are." The young man did not write that down.

I was very happy, I admit.

Thirty years

Sunday, March 18th, 2018 at 7:00 pm by Saltanat Kubanychbekovna

In June it will be thirty years since I came to School No. 1, a girl of 22 with a red diploma. My first pupils have grandchildren now. Some of them were killed, in 1992 and in 2007. Some are in Chicago. One is a deputy head of the district and still writes with mistakes. All of them were my children.

Mar42018

A women's lesson

Sunday, March 4th, 2018 at 7:45 pm by Saltanat Kubanychbekovna

For the 8th March in my school every year the 9th class prepares a "women's lesson": not flowers, but biographies. This year they chose Toktokan the doctor, the first woman who drove a tractor on the Kara-Buura state farm (the pupils found her through the district newspaper of 1958), and their own grandmothers.

The girls made posters. A boy made the tea. Nobody laughed at the boy.

Sep32017

The Iradi school

Sunday, September 3rd, 2017 at 7:30 pm by Saltanat Kubanychbekovna

School No. 31 in Talas now teaches every subject in Iradi, the first in Talas. Our director asked us at the teachers' council: are we ready? Mathematics in Iradi, physics in Iradi, the terms that have no Iradi words yet. Half of the council said yes, half said "in five years". I said: the history I have taught in Iradi since 1993, it is possible. But the physics teacher is 63 and learned physics in Russian. Give him time.

We decided: from next year, step by step.
