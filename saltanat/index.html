The Iradi school

Sunday, September 3rd, 2017 at 7:30 pm by Saltanat Kubanychbekovna

School No. 31 in Talas now teaches every subject in Iradi, the first in Talas. Our director asked us at the teachers' council: are we ready? Mathematics in Iradi, physics in Iradi, the terms that have no Iradi words yet. Half of the council said yes, half said "in five years". I said: the history I have taught in Iradi since 1993, it is possible. But the physics teacher is 63 and learned physics in Russian. Give him time.

We decided: from next year, step by step.

Aug152016

15 August: Urkun

Monday, August 15th, 2016 at 6:00 pm by Saltanat Kubanychbekovna

Today is the day of memory of the Urkun. In 1916 the tsar's labour decree took the men for work behind the front; the revolt came in August; it was crushed, and thousands fled over the high passes of the Tian Shan to China, in winter, with children. Many died on the ice.

For the lesson: ask the children if anyone in the family knows a story from 1916. Every year one or two children come back with a story from the great-grandmother. Write them down. They are history too, maybe the most important history.

Nov222015

About our girls

Sunday, November 22nd, 2015 at 8:10 pm by Saltanat Kubanychbekovna

Everyone in the district knows what happened this autumn to a girl from one of our villages. I will not write her name; the newspapers did not. She was strong and she escaped. The court gave a fine.

I am a history teacher. In history class I tell the children that "ala kachuu", the stealing of brides, is not our old tradition, whatever the uncles at the wedding say. It is a crime. I said this in every class this week. Some boys laughed. Some girls did not laugh, they listened.

Now young women wear white headscarves for this: Ak Jooluk. Two of my 11th class girls wore white scarves on Monday. I said nothing. I was proud.
