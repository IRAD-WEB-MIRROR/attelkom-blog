SEASON 5

Saturday, December 9th, 2017 · 10:00 pm
Next to the club is the Misgaron row. Every year the guys make the cup a new base. This year's base is a piece of an old samovar.

Because nobody asked, I will tell you: all the matches, all the results of five seasons are in a notebook with a green cover. 11,314 rounds. Yes I counted. No, it was not hard.

The league is growing, and the room is not. Dilshat-aka is thinking about a second room, but the neighbour is a barber who says "no noise after 21:00", and a barber has a big influence in the Old City.

SEASON 3

Saturday, December 12th, 2015 · 10:40 pm

16 teams. The wall is not big enough. Dilshat-aka painted a part of the next wall with white paint and said "no more". I drew the bracket on the white paint with a ruler. Everybody is impressed. I'm not good at drawing, but I'm good with a ruler.

Rule change, and why

Sunday, October 18th, 2015 · 8:00 pm

I have been asked many times: "why is the league still on CS 1.6, the club has newer computers?" Short answer: the computers in the back room are 14 old boxes from 2009 with an old video card. They run 1.6 like a dream and the new game like a slideshow. Long answer: 1.6 is the game our brothers played, and the Old City likes things that don't change. Also, our oldest player has been playing since he was 22 and does not want to learn a new one.

The final that never ended

Saturday, February 14th, 2015 · 11:30 pm

Final: Sarchashma United vs Citadel Rush. 14-14 on inferno, last round, the bomb is planted, 4 seconds to the kaboom, and the power cut. All the monitors black. Complete silence. A boy said "don't touch anything!", but nobody was touching anything.

When the light came back (31 minutes later) the match was gone: the server restarted. We had to decide by a coin. Dilshat-aka said "we do not decide a final by a coin". We did a 1v1 on aim_map between the two captains, with knives, at 1 a.m., with 40 people in the room.

Sarchashma United won, the captain of Citadel Rush shook his hand, and told him "you were lucky". It was true.

The girls' team

Saturday, January 10th, 2015 · 9:30 pm

They are called Ak Gül ("white flower"), five girls from the philology building at TSU, one of them is the sister of a Misgaron Boy, who now hates it. They have lost two matches 16-2 and 16-4 and won the third 16-15 in overtime on inferno. The room stood up. I am not joking, all the room stood up and clapped, and the guys they beat clapped too.

The Lagers captain said "she was camping at B". The girl (the captain of Ak Gül) said "yes, I was camping at B and your whole team was running into it". Beautiful.

SEASON 2: 12 TEAMS

Saturday, December 6th, 2014 · 10:10 pm
New bracket on the wall. This time Dilshat-aka gave us the WHOLE wall and a new marker, the thick one.

Registration closed in 4 days: 12 teams. Two are from Nurzhan (they came by the bus on purpose), one team is only girls (!), one team is 14-year-olds who say they are "retired".

New rules after last winter:

  • Maps: dust2, inferno, nuke, train, plus de_cbble if both captains agree

  • The admin (Dilshat-aka or his technician) can stop a match if somebody turns the fan toward another player's keyboard

  • Substitutes allowed, but only if they came at the start

  • If the light goes out: pause the game, count to 300, continue

First round on the 19th.

FINAL: Misgaron Boys vs Temir-Kapka 5

Saturday, February 8th, 2014 · 11:55 pm
The back room at midnight. 30 people watching 10 monitors. You cannot see the floor.

The final was played on a big monitor that Dilshat-aka took from a dead computer in the shop. 31 people in a room for 14 people. A grandmother came in to buy a charger and stayed until the end (she supported Misgaron, because her grandson plays there).

Result: 16-13, MISGARON BOYS. The captain got the copper cup. He hit it with a spoon, it sounded good. Then he hit it with a bigger spoon and put a dent in it. It is now the OFFICIAL dent.

Thank you to everybody. Season 2 in December. The bracket will stay on the wall until then, please nobody wash it.

Semifinals, and the night the power went

Saturday, January 18th, 2014 · 9:40 pm

Semifinal 1 was great. Semifinal 2 was 11-11 when the light went out. All the monitors, the fans, the helicopter. 4 minutes in the dark. Somebody turned on the phone flashlight, somebody started to sing the anthem of Temir-Kapka, which is not an anthem, it is a song about a goat.

Dilshat-aka found the fuse. Light came back, the game was restarted from the same round (we trust each other, mostly), and Temir-Kapka won by one round. The Lagers say it is not fair. Of course it is not fair, it is a LAN in the Old City.

THE LEAGUE STARTS (finally)

Saturday, December 7th, 2013 · 10:30 pm
The back room of Kashkar before anybody comes. 14 monitors, 14 chairs, 1 fan that sounds like a helicopter.

OK people. After 3 weeks of arguing about the rules in the shop, the Old City CS 1.6 League is real. Dilshat-aka said yes, he will give us the LAN on Friday and Saturday nights from 20:00 and the winter is ours. Entry is free, you only pay the normal hourly price (5 som). Night pass 18 for those who want to stay until 7.

8 teams, 5 players each, starting 20 December. Registration is in the shop on a sheet of paper from my school notebook. Names so far:

  • Misgaron Boys (the copper street kids, scary)

  • Temir-Kapka 5

  • Sarchashma United

  • Citadel Rush

  • Kalan Darwaza

  • The Lagers (from the new buildings, they have ADSL at home, so we hate them)

  • two more spots. Hurry up.

Prize: a cup. A copper cup from the Misgaron guys. Not gold. But copper is better because it sounds good when you hit it with a spoon.
