Why your phone says "No network" in the mountains

Sunday, July 11th, 2010 · 9:10 pm

Every summer people come back from the jailoo angry that the phone did not work. It is not the phone. There are no towers on the summer pastures, the sheep don't pay for SIM cards. Climb a hill and stand still. That is the official advice of Ruslan's Gadget Corner.

ADSL at home: one month

Sunday, September 14th, 2008 · 10:15 pm

I got ADSL at home from Attelkom! One month report:

  • Speed: in the morning very fast. In the evening when everybody comes home, like dial-up with ambitions.

  • No more modem noise at night, my mother is very happy.

  • I can download a 4 MB music clip in two minutes. TWO MINUTES.

Verdict: 9/10. Minus 1 for the evening.
