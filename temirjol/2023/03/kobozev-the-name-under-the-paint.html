Kobozev, the name under the paint

budding · planted 2023-03 · tended 2023-03

The side wall of Nurzhan station, the old name faint under the grey.

The city of Nurzhan was called Kobozev until September 1992. On the side of the old station building, under the grey paint, if the sun is low you can still read the old name in tall Russian letters. Nobody has repainted the wall since the new name went up on the roof. Perhaps nobody wants to.

My dispatcher's room is upstairs, in the part of the building that was once the Kobozev division of the regional administration in Alma-Ata. Our files from the Soviet time are still in the cellar. See Stations of the Chu line.
