Kuurdak after the slaughter

21 September 2014 · Toi
Kuurdak in the big pan. My husband's brother did the meat.

Meat in small pieces, liver, kidneys, onion, salt. Fry in its own fat until dark. No water. Serve hot with bread and tea, and a story about the year the sheep escaped.
