My daughter-in-law types my recipes

12 November 2017 · Notes

Chynara sat with me for six Sundays and typed the cards. 212 recipes. Some have no quantities (because "a handful of mine is a handful of mine"), so she measured my hands in her coffee cups.

She says one day she will print a book for the children. She says she needs only the hands. I say the hands will come with the book, free, in the kitchen, on Saturdays.
