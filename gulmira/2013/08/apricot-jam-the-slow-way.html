Apricot jam, the slow way

11 August 2013 · Jam
From the Iyık Janyl road, three buckets.

5 kg apricots, stones out. 4 kg sugar. Layers in the basin, leave overnight. Boil 5 minutes, cool 6 hours. Three times. That's all. Patience is the main ingredient, the sugar is the second.

Crack 20 stones and put the kernels in. My mother always did.
